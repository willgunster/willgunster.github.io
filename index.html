Rating Players from Small Leagues: A Walk-Forward Test on the Belize Domestic League, 2024–26

How much should a team trust a box-score line from a small league, where seasons are short and box scores are partial? This project places lines from the Belize domestic league, three FIBA youth and regional tournaments and Spain’s third division on an estimated NCAA Division I-equivalent BPM scale, then shrinks each player’s short seasons toward a prior, weighted by minutes. Only the domestic next-season prediction was tested.

Result: walk-forward on 102 player-seasons from 55 players, the rating’s RMSE was 2.24 against 3.35 for raw prior-season averages. Against the average of covered players (2.60) the gain was not distinguishable from zero.

NBA Draft · Pre-registered · Oct 2026, rev. Oct 22

Does College Development Predict NBA Value?

Does how a player improves in college tell a team anything beyond his final season and the consensus big board? The test was frozen on August 10, 2026, before any NBA outcome was joined: 17 development features, walk-forward on the 2014–21 classes, and a bar set in advance.

Result: neither model met the bar. Development changed the Spearman correlation with 4-year VORP by +0.01 with gradient boosting and +0.02 with an elastic net, higher in only 4 of 8 classes. The consensus board alone (0.46) ranked prospects as well as the best model (0.47).

Forecasting · Simulator · Oct 2026, rev. Oct 23

How Predictable Is NBA Cup Group Play?

A simulator that turns preseason win totals into team ratings and plays every group under the official Cup tiebreak rules. It reproduces the official 2023–25 results exactly: 60 of 60 placements, 16 of 16 seeds and 12 of 12 quarterfinal pairings.

Result: in a walk-forward backtest of 18 groups, its group-winner forecasts tied the pre-tournament market consensus (log loss 1.359 against 1.370), and its log loss was 15.5% lower than a uniform 1-in-5 guess. The 2026 forecast was frozen on October 14 and will be graded once group play ends on November 27.

What Separates Top-10 Picks? Public Pre-Draft Information and the NBA Draft, 2008–2025

Among 1,073 drafted players, a logistic regression on public pre-draft information separates top-10 picks from picks 11–60 with AUC 0.865, against 0.775 for age alone. Age is the strongest signal: 39.4% of draftees aged 19.5 or younger went in the top 10, against 2.6% of those older than 22.5. Once a post-combine mock draft is known, the model adds nothing measurable.

How Much Late-Game Shooting Carries Over? NBA Play-by-Play, 2012–26

Using win-probability leverage splits and play-by-play from 13,508 games, late-game shot volume measured against role carries over modestly from one season to the next (r = 0.29), well below the 0.85 of ordinary shot volume. Late-game accuracy shows no detectable carryover, and free-throw percentage in high-leverage moments runs 1.20 points below each shooter’s own normal rate.
